Our Family Budget — Quick Guide

The website: the link you were given

← Back to the budget

Getting started

  • Open the link, type the passphrase, tap Unlock.
  • On a phone, add the page to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.
  • The little line at the top tells you if your changes are saved.

🏠 Home

  • Bills due this week are at the top. Tap Mark Paid when you pay one.
  • Shows this month’s income, spending and what’s left over.
  • Savings Goals: tap Add Money as you save.

📊 Budget

  • Type how much you plan to spend in each category.
  • This Pay Period shows what’s due before the next paycheck.
  • Your Bills: tap Mark Paid. Print Bills List makes a big-print copy for the fridge.

🧾 Log

  • Every purchase and paycheck. Tap + Add a Transaction.
  • Tap the ✏️ pencil to fix or delete one.
  • Use Search to find a store or an amount.

💳 Debt

  • Add each debt. Tap Log Payment when you pay it.
  • Debt-Free Date shows about when you’ll be done.

⚙️ Settings

  • Text Size: Normal, Large or Extra large.
  • Download Backup keeps a safe copy of everything.
  • Lock This Device Now if you lend your phone.

Made a mistake?

  • Deleted something? Tap Undo — it shows for 8 seconds.
  • Marked a bill paid by accident? Tap Undo next to it.
  • Tap the ✏️ pencil on anything to change it.

If the top line says…

SyncedAll saved and shared. Nothing to do.
Offline — saved here, will sync laterThat’s fine. It saves to everyone once you have internet.
Token expired, or can’t find the repositoryCall the family tech helper. Nothing is lost.
